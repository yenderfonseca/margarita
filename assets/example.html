Meet the Harbor Mug

A 12 oz stoneware mug with a thumb rest, thrown on the wheel and glazed in batches of forty.

Why it took us a year

We wanted a mug that holds heat without getting heavy. Forty test firings later, the walls are thin at the rim and thick at the base.
